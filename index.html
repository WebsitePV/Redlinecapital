About Us

Redline Capital

Redline Capital specialises in the acquisition and sale of exclusive automobiles. Our curated collection features only the finest machines, selected on authenticity, rarity and condition. Every car in our collection has a story worth telling. We make sure it gets told.

Powered by a passion for extraordinary automobiles and driven to build a legacy, we aim to redefine the art of collecting these extraordinary pieces. Because legacy is not inherited, it is built over time.

The Collection

Centrepiece
Ferrari
F40
1989
The F40 is widely regarded as one of the greatest Ferraris ever built. Conceived by Enzo Ferrari himself, it was the last model he personally approved before his passing. Raw, uncompromising and built purely for the road and track, the F40 set a new benchmark for what a road car could be. Its twin-turbocharged V8, stripped interior and near-total lack of driver aids make it an analogue experience unlike anything built before or since.
Ferrari
Testarossa Nero
1989
The Testarossa defined an era. Its wide, straked flanks and flat-12 engine became icons of 1980s automotive design. This particular example, finished in rare Nero black, stands apart from the predominantly red production run. Few Testarossas exist in this specification, making it a genuinely distinctive piece of Ferrari history.
Ferrari
550 Maranello
1999
The 550 Maranello marked Ferrari's return to the front-engined V12 gran turismo after the mid-engined Testarossa generation. Widely praised by drivers and critics alike, it combined genuine performance with everyday usability — a balance Ferrari had struggled to strike for years. Its naturally aspirated 5.5-litre V12 remains one of the finest engines the company has ever produced.
Ferrari
575 Superamerica
2005
The 575 Superamerica is one of the most technically unusual Ferraris ever built. Its defining feature is an electrochromic rotating glass roof panel — a piece of engineering that allows the car to transform from coupé to open-top in seconds. Produced in only 559 examples, it represents one of the more adventurous chapters in Ferrari's gran turismo story.
Ferrari
GTC4 Lusso
2020
The GTC4 Lusso is Ferrari's most practical car — and that is not a criticism. Its shooting brake body, four-wheel drive system and rear-seat configuration make it the only Ferrari genuinely suited to year-round, everyday use. Beneath the practicality sits a 6.3-litre naturally aspirated V12 that produces 680 horsepower and one of the most memorable engine sounds in the modern Ferrari range.
Ferrari
Roma Spider
2024
The Roma Spider takes its name and spirit from the golden age of Italian la dolce vita. Where many modern Ferraris lean into aggression, the Roma takes a different path — one of understated elegance and effortless style. Its turbocharged V8 and retractable hard top make it one of the most complete open-top Ferraris in recent memory.
Ferrari
12 Cilindri Coupé
2025
The 12 Cilindri represents Ferrari's commitment to the naturally aspirated V12 at a time when the industry is moving away from large displacement engines. Its 6.5-litre unit produces 830 horsepower and revs to 9,500 rpm — figures that place it among the most extreme road-going V12s ever made. A car that will be looked back on as the last of its kind.
